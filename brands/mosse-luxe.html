Caring for Your Leather

Cleaning

Gently wipe with a dry, soft cloth. For deeper cleaning, use a damp cloth with mild soap.

Conditioning

Apply quality leather conditioner every 3-6 months to maintain suppleness.

Storage

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Use dust bags provided.

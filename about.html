The Mauwane Legacy

Founded in 2023, Mauwane Legacy Collective carries forward the craftsmanship traditions established by Moses Mauwane, whose dedication to quality became legendary in his community.

"True craftsmanship isn't about perfection, but about putting your soul into every piece."

— Moses Mauwane

Our Core Values

Artisanal Craftsmanship

We cherish the timeless art of handmade creations. Every stitch, every detail speaks of a craft passed down through generations, blending tradition with modern artistry to create something truly unique.

Creativity That Connects

At the heart of everything we do is creativity. We create to connect—whether through leather goods or educational tools, our goal is to inspire imagination, spark curiosity, and build bonds within our community.

Legacy in Motion

We aren’t just a business; we’re continuing a legacy. A legacy of craftsmanship, meaningful stories, and a future where creativity, culture, and collaboration define us.

Passion in Every Detail

In everything we create, passion runs through every thread. From the spark of an idea to the final product, each step is made with precision, purpose, and the belief that the little things make all the difference.

Our Journey

1987

Where It Started

Rapudi Moses Mauwane left his job and began making leather belts from home to support his family. He worked with care, using traditional skills to craft high-quality products that lasted.

2023

A New Chapter Begins

Mauwane Legacy Collective was founded to carry that legacy forward. Today, we bring together handmade ideas under one roof—from premium leather goods through Mossé Luxe to creative educational tools with LSG Creations, along with upcoming brands, business ideas, and new opportunities.

Today

Shaping a Vision for Tomorrow

We’re laying the foundation for a collective driven by the power of legacy, the spark of creativity, and the strength of purpose.

Meet the Collective

Olerato Mauwane

Founder, Creative Director & Leather Crafter

Olerato Mauwane is the founder of Mauwane Legacy Collective and the driving force behind its creative direction. With a strong background in graphic design and IT, Olerato brings a modern approach to building a business rooted in tradition. He combines digital creativity with hands-on craftsmanship to create meaningful, high-quality products.

Olerato is also the skilled crafter behind Mossé Luxe, designing and handmaking luxury leather goods that reflect timeless style and exceptional detail. Inspired by his late grandfather's legacy, he continues the family’s tradition of working with leather—reimagining it for today’s audience. His passion lies in creating products with purpose, while slowly growing a collective that brings together heritage, innovation, and opportunity.

Lesego Setshedi

Co-Founder, Lead Designer & Fashion Creative

Lesego Setshedi is the co-founder of Mauwane Legacy Collective and the creative mind behind LSG Creations. With a background in fashion design and a deep love for education and creativity, Lesego designs tools and décor that inspire young minds. Her work focuses on blending learning with beauty—making educational resources that are both playful and practical.

Beyond LSG Creations, Lesego brings her fashion expertise and design thinking to future projects within the collective. She is also skilled in leather crafting and contributes to the making of handcrafted goods under Mossé Luxe. Her goal is to use creativity to make a lasting impact—especially in spaces where learning and culture come together.

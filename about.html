The Mauwane Legacy

Mauwane Legacy Collective started in 2023 with a simple goal — to create with purpose, style, and heart. We are proud to carry forward the love for handmade work, where every product is made with care and creativity.

Our Core Values

Artisanal Craftsmanship

We value the timeless skill of handmade work. Every stitch and every detail shows care, combining tradition and modern design to create something truly special.

Creativity That Connects

Creativity is at the core of what we do. Whether it's leather pieces or learning tools, we design to inspire, encourage curiosity, and bring people together.

Legacy in Motion

We’re more than a business—we’re building a legacy. One shaped by craft, culture, and community. We honor the past while moving forward with purpose and collaboration.

Our Journey

1987

Where It Started

Rapudi Moses Mauwane left his job and began making leather belts from home to support his family. He worked with care, using traditional skills to craft high-quality products that lasted.

2023

A New Chapter Begins

Mauwane Legacy Collective was founded to carry that legacy forward. Today, we bring together handmade ideas under one roof—from premium leather goods through Mossé Luxe to creative educational tools with LSG Creations, along with upcoming brands, business ideas, and new opportunities.

Today

Shaping a Vision for Tomorrow

We’re laying the foundation for a collective driven by the power of legacy, the spark of creativity, and the strength of purpose.

More Than Brands — We Are a Creative Hub

Mauwane Legacy Collective is more than Mossé Luxe and LSG Creations. We are a home for makers, dreamers, and collaborators who believe in the power of legacy and originality.

A Space for Creativity & Collaboration

We welcome new ideas, upcoming brands, artistic minds, and business opportunities. Mauwane Legacy Collective is a place where tradition meets innovation — where we’re building a future shaped by creativity, community, and purpose.

Whether you’re an artist, designer, educator, or entrepreneur, our doors are open. Let’s build something meaningful, together.

Meet the Collective

Olerato Mauwane

Founder, Creative Director & Leather Crafter

Olerato Mauwane is the founder of Mauwane Legacy Collective and leads its creative vision. With a strong background in graphic design and IT, he brings fresh ideas to a business rooted in heritage. He blends digital skills with handmade craftsmanship to create meaningful, high-quality products.

Olerato also leads Mossé Luxe, where he designs and handcrafts luxury leather goods with timeless style and fine detail. Inspired by his family's legacy, he reimagines traditional leatherwork for today. His passion is creating products that have purpose, while growing a collective focused on creativity, culture, and opportunity.

Lesego Setshedi

Co-Founder, Lead Designer & Fashion Creative

Lesego Setshedi is the co-founder of Mauwane Legacy Collective and the founder of LSG Creations. She brings a background in fashion design and a passion for education. Lesego creates learning tools and décor that are fun, inspiring, and practical for young minds.

As a fashion creative and leather crafter, she also works on handcrafted pieces under Mossé Luxe. Her goal is to use creativity to make a positive impact—especially in spaces where learning and culture come together.
